01

Natural Semantic Query & Episodic Recall Breakdown

ISSUE-4091 (Critical)
Focus: Cognitive search anchors, chronological decay, and query abandonment paths
5 Questions keyboard_arrow_down
psychology
Research Objective

Unpack how users mentally structure recall queries when attempting to retrieve photos older than 18 months, identifying exact inflection points between episodic cues (e.g., emotions, relational memories) and algorithmic tags.

1

Can you walk me through the last time you searched for a specific photo and couldn't find it immediately?

↳

Probe: What specific details or "cues" did you type into the search bar first (names, dates, location, activity)?

↳

Probe: When the first set of results appeared, what caused you to abandon or refine the search?

↳

Probe: Did you try searching on another platform (like WhatsApp media or Instagram chats) instead?

2

How do you expect Google Photos to understand relative time descriptions like "summer before college" or "when Sarah had short hair"?

↳

Probe: Have you ever noticed search results showing random photos from the wrong year?

↳

Probe: If an assistant asked you clarifying questions during search, how would that feel?

assignment_turned_in Live Task Scenario (Observational Prompt)

Ask participant to open Google Photos right now. Prompt: "Please search for a photo of a family meal from two years ago where someone was laughing." Observe search query iterations, pause duration, and facial micro-expressions of frustration.

02

Silent Cloud Backup Stalls & Storage Quota Anxiety

ISSUE-3890 (Critical)

Probing user awareness of backup failure indicators, sync lag, and recovery behavior after phone loss.

4 Questions keyboard_arrow_down
cloud_off
Research Objective

Evaluate participant mental models regarding when local content is actually secure in the cloud, and test panic thresholds when encountering cryptic sync failure icons.

1. When did you last verify that your photos from today are completely backed up?

↳ Probe: Do you actively look for the spinning circle or checkmark icon?

2. What happens in your mind when your Google Storage alert reaches 95% full?

↳ Probe: Have you ever deleted local files hoping to free Google Drive cloud space?

03

Facial Recognition Drift & Erroneous People Merging

ISSUE-3712 (High)

Investigating emotional response when unrelated individuals or twins are merged into a single person tag.

5 Questions keyboard_arrow_down
face
Research Objective

Discover manual tagging exhaustion thresholds and examine user patience with algorithmic misidentification across diverse skin tones and aging timelines.

1. Have you ever seen two different people grouped under the same face album?

↳ Probe: How easy or difficult was it to unmerge them?
